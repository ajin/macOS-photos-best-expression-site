First run

  1. Launch Best Expression once. This registers the extension with macOS; the app itself is just the front door.
  2. Click Grant Photo Library Access when asked, or grant it later when the extension asks from inside Photos.
  3. In Photos, open a photo and click Edit, then the ••• button in the top right toolbar, then Best Expression.

The extension is missing from the ••• menu

  1. Make sure you launched the app at least once.
  2. Open System Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions → Photos Editing and switch on Best Expression.
  3. Quit Photos fully and reopen it. Photos reads its extension list at launch.

“No other shots found”

The search looks for photos taken near the same moment. Widen the search window in the sidebar (it reaches out to six hours), or click Add From Library… and hand-pick any shots of the same person. Photos stored in iCloud download on demand, so a slow connection makes the first scan take longer.

A face won't match across shots

The matcher needs the same person at a roughly similar position and size. Add references taken closer in time, or use Add From Library… and pick the shots yourself. If a face never got detected at all, right-click it and choose Add Face Here.

Live Photos

Saving an edit keeps the edited still and switches off the motion, as with every third-party Photos editor. Revert to Original brings the motion back. Duplicate the photo in Photos before editing if you want a moving copy and an edited copy side by side.

Undo everything

Edits are non-destructive. In Photos: Image → Revert to Original. The untouched shot comes back, whenever, always.

Requirements

macOS 14 or later on an Apple silicon Mac. Everything runs on device with no network connections; see the privacy policy, which fits on a postcard.

Report a bug

Write what you clicked, what you expected, and what happened instead, plus your macOS version. Screenshots help. Send nothing you would mind a stranger seeing; a screenshot of the sliders is plenty, the photo itself is rarely needed. Email or GitHub, whichever you prefer.
