Privacy policy

Last updated August 17, 2026

Best Expression does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Best Expression edits photos entirely on your Mac. It makes no network connections: there are no servers, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no accounts.

Photo library access serves one purpose: finding other shots taken around the same time as the photo you are editing, so you can swap in a better expression. Your photos, and everything computed from them (face positions, quality scores), stay on your Mac.

Questions: write to the address on the support page.

That is the whole policy. There is no long version.
