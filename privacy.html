Privacy policy

Last updated August 21, 2026

Best Expression does not collect, store on any server, or transmit any personal data. Everything it does happens on your Mac.

The app has no network connections, no servers, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no accounts.

Photo library access

The app asks for access to your photo library for one purpose: finding other shots taken around the same time as the photo you are editing, so you can swap in a better expression. It reads photos only while you are editing, and it saves edits through Apple Photos as standard non-destructive adjustments.

Face data

To do its job the app analyzes faces in your photos, entirely on your Mac. This section explains exactly what that involves.

What is processed. While you edit a photo, the app detects faces and computes, in memory: face bounding boxes, facial landmark points, expression scores (smile and eye openness), image quality scores, and short numeric image signatures used only to match the same person across the few shots of the same moment. It also computes hair and person segmentation masks used for blending. All of this is derived with Apple's Vision framework and open-source models running locally on your Mac. None of it is used to identify who a person is, and the app keeps no database of faces.

Storage. This in-memory face data exists only for the duration of the editing session and is discarded when you close the editor. It is never written to a server. When you save an edit, Apple Photos stores the app's edit recipe inside your own photo library as non-destructive adjustment data, the same mechanism every Photos editing extension uses. That recipe contains only: which of your photos a face was taken from, the normalized rectangle of the face within the photo, and your slider settings. It contains no landmark points, no image signatures, no biometric templates, and no face images. Because it lives inside your photo library, it stays wherever your library is: on your Mac, and in iCloud Photos if you have that turned on, under your own Apple account.

Sharing. Face data is not shared with anyone: not with us, not with third parties, not with the developers of the open-source models. The app makes no network connections at all.

Retention and deletion. In-memory face data is deleted when the editing session ends. The saved edit recipe persists as long as the edit exists. Choosing Revert to Original in Photos deletes it, as does deleting the photo. Uninstalling the app does not remove recipes already stored in your library, because Photos keeps your edits; Revert to Original removes them at any time.

Third-party components

The app bundles open-source face analysis models from the MediaPipe project and the TensorFlow Lite runtime, both under the Apache 2.0 license. They run entirely on your Mac and send nothing anywhere. License notices are in the app's Acknowledgements screen.

Children

The app has no accounts and collects no data, including from children.
