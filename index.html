Face swap for group photos

Someone always blinks.

The best smile lands in shot two, the open eyes in shot four; the photo you wanted is spread across five files. Best Expression is a face swap extension inside Apple Photos on your Mac: click the blinked face, borrow that person's better take from a shot seconds away, and save the photo you meant to take. Every pixel comes from your own shots. Nothing leaves your Mac.

How it works

Step 01

Open the shot

Any photo already in your library. In Photos: Edit, then the ••• button top right, then Best Expression. Installing is the same motion as Pixelmator or Affinity: launch the app once and the extension registers itself.

Step 02

Click a face

Every face gets an outline. Click one and the extension finds that person's other takes in shots from the same moment, scores them for sharpness, open eyes and smile, and lines them up best first. Frames inside Live Photos count too.

Step 03

Pick the take

One click blends it in: the face is fitted, the colors are matched, and the settings tune themselves in about a second. Nudge the sliders if you want. Save Changes writes a full-quality edit that Revert to Original can always undo.

The blend

A pasted-on face is easy to spot, so most of the work went into the part where you can't tell. Every pixel still comes from your own shots; nothing is invented.

  • Fit The new face is reshaped to sit exactly where the old one sat, jawline and all.
  • Blend Color and light are matched to the rest of the photo, so no edge or tone shift gives it away.
  • Hair Hairlines stay untouched on both people. A wrong hairline is what ruins most face swaps.
  • Bursts Shots taken seconds apart can swap the whole head, hair and glasses included, with the background left alone.
  • Check The extension grades its own result and keeps adjusting until the swap blends in. The sliders stay there if you want the last word.

Privacy

Your photos stay home.

No network code

Best Expression makes no network connections. No analytics, no crash reporting, no account, no subscription. The App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected because there is nothing to collect.

ML, kept on a leash

Machine learning does exactly one job here: finding faces and their landmarks, on your Mac. Every pixel in a result comes from your own photos.

Reversible, always

Edits save non-destructively through the Photos extension system. Revert to Original restores the untouched shot, even months later.

Questions

What does a swap need?

At least one other photo of the same person taken close in time. A burst or two consecutive shots works best. The Tweak Face sliders are the exception: they work on any single photo with a face in it.

Can it open fully closed eyes without another shot?

No. The sliders reshape the face's own pixels and they stop at subtle. Fully closed eyes need a real take to borrow from. Nothing here invents an eyeball.

What happens with Live Photos?

Two things. Their video frames serve as extra takes, filtered down to the sharp ones. And when you save an edit over a Live Photo, Photos keeps the edited still and switches off the motion, as it does for every third-party editor. Duplicate the photo first if you want a live copy too. Revert to Original brings the motion back.

Where does it show up?

Launch the app once after installing, then in Photos: Edit, the ••• button top right, Best Expression.

Missing from the menu? System Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions → Photos Editing, switch it on, restart Photos. The support page walks through it.

What does it cost?

One price, paid once. No subscription, no tiers, no account. The number lands with the App Store listing.

Which Macs?

macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon. The pipeline leans on Core Image and the Neural Engine, so it stays quick on full-resolution photos.
